AI automation · 15,500 subscribers

The four biggest videos on this channel all name a specific software tool in the title. They did 63,523, 57,682, 45,687 and 24,165 views.

The two most recent uploads are interviews, titled around who the guest is. They did 114 and 76.

The interviews aren't the problem. This audience arrived for a named tool and something they could go and build afterwards. A guest's name carries no search demand and makes no promise, so the video has to be sold entirely on the thumbnail. Same guest, framed as a specific build, is a different video.

11,746 → 158
Lawn care · 16,700 subscribers

Median across the last 43 long-form uploads: 194 views. One video did 11,746 — a profile of a teenager running a lawn care route. Another did 5,012, following a guy who went from working out of a car trunk to 62 lawns a week.

In the same month, two motivational uploads about not waiting and not being late did 187 and 158.

The winners put a real person and a real number in front. The motivational ones ask a stranger to care about the message before they know what's inside. Sixty times apart, same channel, same weeks.

Not every stalled channel is a big one.

What a concept looks like

Current
4 Critical Business Decisions That Build Your Dream Landscaping Company!
2,100 views · channel median 6,600
Proposed
$5 Million Without Leaving a 5-Mile Radius
Reasoning

A 251,000 subscriber lawn care channel. Median across the last fifteen uploads: 6,600 views. This one did 2,100.

The thumbnail is good. It promises four strategies he wishes he'd done sooner — regret, specificity, a reason to click. The title promises four critical decisions that build your dream company, which is an abstraction, and nobody is standing in an abstraction. The packaging is making two different arguments and the weaker one is the part search sees.

Twenty minutes in, he says he wants to build a $5 million company inside a five-mile radius, and that he turns down work outside it. That's the video. Neither the title nor the thumbnail knows it's there.

The proposed title makes the same claim the video already makes. Nothing was added.
